FORMULA

(2π/T)² = gk tanh(kh), λ = 2π/k

Assumptions

Required: small-amplitude linear gravity waves over a flat bottom with uniform depth, incompressible inviscid irrotational flow and no mean current. Excluded: surface tension, breaking, strong nonlinearity, capillary waves and rapidly varying depth.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
